Our Healthy Recipes

Choose from our collection of delicious food ideas.

Healthy Breakfast

Oats, fruits and nuts for a balanced start.

Time: 10 minutes

Calories: About 300 per serving

Ingredients: Oats, milk, banana and nuts.

Steps: Cook oats in milk. Add banana and nuts.

Fresh Vegetable Salad

A refreshing mix of vegetables.

Time: 10 minutes

Calories: About 180 per serving

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot and lemon.

Steps: Wash, chop and mix all ingredients.

Fruit Smoothie

A refreshing drink made with fresh fruit.

Time: 5 minutes

Calories: About 200 per serving

Ingredients: Banana, strawberries and milk.

Steps: Blend all ingredients until smooth.

Vegetable Soup

A warm meal with a variety of vegetables.

Time: 25 minutes

Calories: About 150 per serving

Ingredients: Carrot, beans, peas and spices.

Steps: Cook vegetables in water with mild spices.

Dal and Rice

A simple home-style meal with protein and carbohydrates.

Time: 30 minutes

Calories: About 350 per serving

Ingredients: Lentils, rice, turmeric and spices.

Steps: Cook lentils and rice separately. Serve together.

Chicken and Vegetables

A meal combining protein and vegetables.

Time: 30 minutes

Calories: About 350 per serving

Ingredients: Chicken, carrots, beans and spices.

Steps: Cook chicken thoroughly and serve with vegetables.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Include fruits and vegetables in your meals.
  • Eat a variety of foods to get different nutrients.
  • Include protein-rich foods in your daily meals.
  • Try to eat regular, balanced meals.
  • Wash fruits and vegetables before eating.
  • Plan your meals before going grocery shopping.
